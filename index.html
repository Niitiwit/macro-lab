Calculation & coaching notes

Estimated resting metabolic rate (RMR/BMR): Mifflin–St Jeor, 10 × kg + 6.25 × cm − 5 × age + 5 (male) or −161 (female). TDEE = RMR × activity factor. Calories = TDEE × (1 + adjustment). Protein and fat are weight-based; carbs fill remaining energy. These are starting estimates, not measured metabolism. BMI is a screening metric, not body fat.

Cut default −15%, recomp −5%, bulk +8%, maintenance 0%. Protein defaults 2.2, 2.0, 1.8 and 1.8 g/kg respectively. Fat defaults 0.8 g/kg. These are practical presets, not universal prescriptions. Review 2–3 weeks of consistent intake, weight trend, waist and training performance before adjusting. Avoid aggressive restriction; persistent fatigue, poor recovery or dizziness warrant reassessment.

Mifflin et al., 1990 · ISSN protein position stand · ISSN diets and body composition
